About Play Different

Learn more about the purpose of Play Different and how it helps users discover modern board games.

Our Mission

Play Different is a website designed to introduce users to modern board games that go beyond traditional titles such as Monopoly or Uno. Many people are unaware of the wide variety of creative, strategic, and engaging games available today.

This website helps users discover new board games by providing key information such as number of players, difficulty level, and average play time. The goal is to make it easier for beginners and hobby players to explore new gaming experiences.

Users can browse games, filter them based on difficulty, and save their favorite games using the favorites feature. This makes it simple to keep track of games they are interested in trying.

This project was developed as part of the WDD131 course to demonstrate skills in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, including responsive design, dynamic content, and localStorage usage.
